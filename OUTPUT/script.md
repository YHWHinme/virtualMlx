# Cold Call Script: Website Services for Dog Shelters

## Goal
To schedule a brief website audit or a discovery call with the shelter manager to discuss how a better website can increase adoptions and donations.

## The Script

**1. The Opening**
"Hi [Manager's Name], this is [Your Name] from [Your Company]. I'm calling because I'm a huge fan of the work you're doing at [Shelter Name], and I actually spent a few minutes on your website this morning."

**2. The "Hook" (Value Proposition)**
"I noticed a few areas where a few small tweaks could really help you get more eyes on your adoptable dogs and potentially increase your online donations. For example, [mention one specific thing, e.g., 'making the adoption form easier to fill out on mobile' or 'adding a clearer "Donate Now" button']."

**3. The Discovery Question**
"I'm curious, is increasing your online visibility or streamlining your adoption process something you're focusing on this year?"

**4. The Call to Action (The Ask)**
"I'd love to put together a free, 5-minute video audit of your site to show you exactly where those opportunities are—no strings attached. Would you be open to me sending that over, or perhaps a brief 10-minute chat next Tuesday morning to walk through it?"

---

## Handling Common Objections

**Objection: "We don't have a budget for this."**
*Response:* "I completely understand. That's actually why I want to send the free audit first. Even if you can't hire someone right now, you'll have a roadmap of exactly what to fix to help more dogs find homes. Would that be helpful?"

**Objection: "We already have a website/someone who handles it."**
*Response:* "That's great! Most shelters do. I'm not looking to replace what you have, but rather to provide a fresh set of eyes to see if there are any 'blind spots' that might be costing you donations. A second opinion never hurts, right?"

**Objection: "I'm too busy right now."**
*Response:* "I totally get it—I know how hectic things are at the shelter. I'll be very brief. Could I just grab your email address to send that free audit over so you can look at it whenever you have a spare moment?"
