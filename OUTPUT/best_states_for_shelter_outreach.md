# Cold Calling Strategy: Animal Shelter & Rescue Outreach (London-Based)

This document organizes the best U.S. and U.K. targets based on their volume of shelters and the optimal calling times from London (GMT/BST).

## U.K. Targets (Day Cycle)
*Focus on independent, volunteer-led, and small-scale rescues. Avoid large networks like RSPCA or Dogs Trust.*

- **South West**: Target small rescues in **Devon, Cornwall, and Somerset**. These rural areas often have many independent, family-run sanctuaries.
- **The Midlands**: Look for independent rescues around **Birmingham, Nottingham, and Leicester**.
- **East Anglia**: Focus on small-scale rescue groups in **Norfolk, Suffolk, and Cambridgeshire**.
- **Local Independent Rescues**: Search for "small dog rescue [City/County]" to find volunteer-run organizations.
- **Private Sanctuaries**: Look for specialized animal sanctuaries that operate on a smaller, non-corporate scale.

## Day Shift (Target: U.S. East Coast / Central)
*Call these in your afternoon (approx. 2 PM – 6 PM GMT) to hit their 9 AM – 1 PM window.*

- **Florida**: Extremely high volume of animal welfare organizations.
- **Georgia**: Strong presence of both municipal shelters and private rescues.
- **Ohio**: Significant number of reporting organizations.
- **Texas**: One of the highest concentrations of shelters and rescues nationwide.
- **Indianapolis (Indy), Indiana**: A strong regional hub in the Midwest.
- **Maine**: High density of shelters per capita.

## Night Shift (Target: U.S. West Coast & Mountain)
*Call these in your evening (approx. 5 PM – 10 PM GMT) to hit their 9 AM – 2 PM window.*

- **California**: A massive market with a high number of reporting rescues.
- **Arizona**: High volume of intake and many rescue organizations.
- **Washington**: Significant number of shelters and rescue groups in the Pacific Northwest.
- **Oregon**: Strong animal welfare community and high rescue activity.
- **Colorado**: A major hub for animal rescues in the Mountain time zone.

## Summary of Target Types
When searching for leads, prioritize **volunteer-based** and **independent** organizations over large corporate charities. Look for:
- **Small Animal Rescues**: Often run by a few dedicated volunteers.
- **Independent Shelters**: Local non-profits without national affiliations.
- **Private Sanctuaries**: Smaller, specialized rescue centers.
